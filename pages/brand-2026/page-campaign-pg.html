Study a Masters at Stirling

Stirling is the perfect place to start your next adventure. Choose from over 90 highly-rated on-campus or online Masters courses, and earn your degree alongside students from more than 140 different countries.

  • Scottish University of the Year and UK Sports University of the Year. (1)
  • 1st in the UK, top 10 in the world for campus environment. (2)
  • Top 40 in the UK for skilled graduate outcomes. (3)
  • Scotland's University for Sporting Excellence and 1st in the UK for sports facilities. (2)
  • We'll guarantee you an offer of university-managed accommodation in your first year.
  • Explore start dates in September or January and find your perfect course.
  • Stirling is the safest student city in the UK. (4)

(1) Daily Mail University Guide 2027 (2) The International Student Barometer 2024 (3) HESA Graduate Outcomes Survey 2023/24 (4) The Complete University Guide 2025

Postgraduate loans of up to £13,900

Tuition fee loans of up to £7,000 are available for eligible full-time and part-time postgraduate courses from Student Awards Agency Scotland. Living-cost loans of up to £6,900 also available.

See funding available

Postgraduate Merit Scholarship

Any UK student with a First Class Honours degree (or equivalent) can apply for a £2,000 scholarship to study full-time on any eligible taught Masters course.

Learn more

Stirling Alumni Scholarship

We offer a discount of 20% for the first year of a full-time or part-time on-campus Masters for UK students who completed their undergraduate degree at Stirling.

Find out more

£2,000 Scottish scholarship

Our 60th anniversary scholarship gives students in Scotland the opportunity to secure a £2,000 tuition fee waiver for a wide range of Masters courses in 2027/28.

See our 60 for 60 scholarship

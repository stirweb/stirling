Study a Masters degree in Psychology

Our Psychology postgraduate courses combine research methods training with more specialised advanced modules and practical placements, preparing you for a successful career in Psychology.

MSc Psychology (accredited conversion course)

Starting September or January

  • For students who have a degree in another subject but want to convert to psychology.
  • Accredited by the British Psychological Society (BPS).
  • Includes a one month work placement.

MSc Health Psychology

Starting September

  • Accredited by the British Psychological Society (BPS): Meets BPS requirements for Stage One training towards professional Health Psychologist status.
  • Learn to use your knowledge of psychology to promote general wellbeing.
  • The course has a strong experiential focus.

MSc Psychology of Sport

Starting September

  • The only sports psychology Masters in Scotland accredited by the British Psychological Society.
  • Learn from leading sports psychology experts.
  • Develop a critical understanding of contemporary sports psychology theory and research.

MSc Psychological Research Methods

Starting September

  • Combines training in psychological theory with practical research skills development.
  • Our flexible course lets you make specialised subject choices to benefit your future career.
  • Develop work-based employment skills on a placement.

Other courses you may be interested in

  • MSc / MA Human-Animal Interaction
  • MSc Autism and Neurodevelopmental Conditions Research
